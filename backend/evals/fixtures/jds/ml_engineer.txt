Senior Machine Learning Engineer — Recommendations Team — StreamMedia

About us
StreamMedia is a streaming platform serving 8M monthly users across 14 countries. The Recommendations team owns the algorithms that decide what every user sees on the home page. We are a 5-person team, fully remote, Europe-only.

What you will do
- Own end-to-end ML projects: dataset, model, offline eval, online A/B, monitoring.
- Iterate on our two-tower retrieval model in PyTorch and our gradient-boosted ranker.
- Improve the offline evaluation suite — currently the bottleneck for shipping speed.
- Partner with data engineering on Spark feature pipelines and with infra on inference latency.
- Mentor 1 mid-level engineer.

Requirements
- 5+ years in production ML, ideally on recommendations, search, or ranking.
- Deep Python and PyTorch.
- Comfort with offline-eval design (ranking metrics, partial credit, fairness).
- Experience with at least one feature store and one experiment platform.
- Strong written communication — we run async design reviews.

Bonus

- Published work or strong open-source contributions.
- Experience scaling models to >100M users.
