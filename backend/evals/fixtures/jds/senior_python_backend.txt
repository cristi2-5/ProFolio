Senior Backend Engineer (Python) — RemoteFirst SA

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own the next generation of our orders + billing platform. You will join a 6-person backend squad and report to the engineering manager. The role is fully remote within the EU.

What you will do
- Design and ship FastAPI services backed by PostgreSQL and Redis.
- Own the migration from our legacy Django monolith to event-driven microservices on Kafka.
- Set the bar on observability — Prometheus metrics, structured logs, runbooks.
- Mentor 2-3 mid-level engineers; lead architecture reviews for cross-team initiatives.
- Be on a humane on-call rotation (1 week / 6).

What we need
- 5+ years building production Python backends; deep FastAPI or Django expertise.
- Strong PostgreSQL — query planning, indexing strategy, schema migrations under load.
- Comfortable with async patterns, idempotency, and message-driven systems (Kafka or NATS).
- Track record of mentoring and influence beyond your direct team.
- Excellent written communication — we document decisions in ADRs.

Nice to have
- Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform.
- Background in payments, billing, or anything with strict correctness requirements.
- Open-source contributions.

What we offer
- Fully remote, EU-only.
- €70-90k base depending on level and location.
- Annual learning budget, conference attendance, work-from-anywhere weeks.
