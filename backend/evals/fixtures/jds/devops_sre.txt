Senior Site Reliability Engineer — Platform Team — CloudOps

Why this role exists
CloudOps runs payment infrastructure for 200+ merchants across the EU. Reliability is the product. We are growing the platform team from 4 to 7 to handle the upcoming multi-region expansion.

What you will do
- Operate and evolve our Kubernetes platform (EKS, 4 clusters, ~600 services).
- Lead the multi-region active-active migration during the next 12 months.
- Drive the GitOps adoption (ArgoCD) for the 3 remaining product teams not yet on it.
- Improve our SLO framework — currently SLIs are inconsistent across services.
- Take part in the on-call rotation (1 week / 5).

Must have
- 6+ years operating production Kubernetes at non-trivial scale.
- Strong Terraform — we treat infrastructure changes as code reviews.
- Comfort with at least one of: Go, Python, Bash, for platform tooling.
- Solid Prometheus / Grafana / alerting story.
- Experience leading or coaching incident response.

Nice to have
- CKA / AWS DevOps Pro certifications.
- Experience with multi-region or multi-cluster failover.
- Background in regulated environments (PCI DSS, SOC2).

Compensation
- €80-110k base.
- Remote, EU-only.
- €3000 annual training budget. On-call paid time off.
